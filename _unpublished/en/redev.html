Who this course is for

For designers and for everyone who works with the numbers

The program is designed for people who work with existing buildings and sites. Everyone takes the common modules, while the practice and the final document depend on the track you choose. You make the final choice in the introductory module; in the application, a preliminary choice is enough.

This is for you if you are

  • a student: you do not need a higher education degree to enroll, you can join right away;
  • an early-career architect, concept designer, or environmental designer;
  • a specialist in an analysis, design, or development department, including at a large private company;
  • a property owner or investor deciding what to do with a building or a plot;
  • a realtor or commercial real estate broker preparing a property for sale or lease with a new use;
  • you want a project you can stand behind with numbers: before a review panel, a client, an investor, or a buyer.

Track A · Designer

Architect, urban planner, designer

You translate functional performance into space-planning solutions. You work more deeply with generative AI models (ComfyUI, ControlNet) to sketch facades and interiors without compromising the structural frame.

Deliverable for Demo Day

An architectural-visual study: a concept for renewing the environment, the building's siting on the terrain, adaptive reuse sketches, functional diagrams.

Track B · Developer and investor

Developer, investor, property owner, realtor, analyst, economist

You turn ideas into numbers and a product. You work more deeply with language models: analyzing land use and development rules, calculating Highest and Best Use (HBU), a zoning matrix, a basic financial model.

Deliverable for Demo Day

An investment concept brief for the property: a product hypothesis, an audience profile, a floor area balance, basic economics, and concept renders. You can use it as the basis for a conversation with a partner, an investor, or a buyer.

Team option. At the final assessment, you can pair up as an architect plus an analyst or developer and present a comprehensive master plan together.

About the program · the problems it solves

Three places where redevelopment most often stalls

01

Training for empty sites

Architectural education focuses on new construction on vacant land and hardly teaches how to work with outdated buildings, former factories, and public spaces.

02

Context is assembled by hand

Weeks go into collecting data, and the property's use is then hard to justify to an investor. In this program, the context and the rationale are assembled with data and language models.

03

Pictures without structure

Mass AI courses teach random image generation and ignore load-bearing walls, column spacing, and regulations. Here the concept rests on the structural frame and building codes.

What you do in the program

A

Test the idea against data

You analyze regulations and codes with language models, describe the audience of the place, and find unmet demand.

B

Calculate functions and floor areas

You determine the Highest and Best Use of the property and assemble a zoning matrix and a floor area balance. For developers, basic economics is added.

C

Present the concept

You make mood boards, adaptive reuse facade sketches that preserve the structural frame, and assemble the final document. Experts review the projects remotely, based on the final documents.

Learning outcomes
After the final review, you keep a ready concept with a rationale and skills you can use from then on.

A project based on a real property

An architectural-visual study or an investment concept brief for the chosen property, backed by calculations and visuals. Suitable for a portfolio, a thesis, or a conversation with an investor, a client, or a buyer.

Skills

  • a rapid audit of the urban environment;
  • scenario-based functional zoning of properties in outdated buildings;

Working toolkit

What sets the program apart

How the program differs from ordinary AI courses

01

A real property

You work on your own real property. If you do not have one, work with the base property C52.

02

Two tracks

Designers and developers follow a common core, while the practice and the final document are specific to each track.

03

A group of five

A small project lab: every project is reviewed individually, not briefly in a general cohort.

04

Language models at the center

Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini calculate and build the argument: development rules, highest and best use, floor area balance, investment copy. Image AI models are secondary.

05

No coding

Ready-made prompt libraries and clear web services. The expert demonstrates ComfyUI and ControlNet at an overview level; you do not need to set them up yourself.

06

Data is ready

QGIS is not part of the program: the site data has been prepared, and you only need to load it into your work. No QGIS knowledge is required.

Besides language models, you use Notion and Gamma to assemble the developer booklet and the pitch deck, and Flux, Midjourney, and ComfyUI for quick illustrations of hypotheses. You need active accounts in the services and a stable network connection. Students independently provide access to the services required for learning in accordance with their user agreements and applicable law. The organizer does not provide communication tools or distribute tools for bypassing blocks.

How the course runs

Group meetings, recorded lectures, and a review of your project

You work on one project from start to finish. Group meetings are for discussing questions and work; lectures can be watched as recordings.

Meetings. Attendance is mandatory. A synchronous video call in Telemost: we discuss questions and the group's work and demonstrate working workflows.

Lectures. Attendance is optional. The recording appears in the course guide once it is ready. Short recordings showing work with models and templates.

Individual work. The group has five people, so every project is reviewed personally.

Chat and templates. A private group chat, materials, and ready-made prompt libraries.

Workload: 72 academic hours over 4.5 weeks. That is about 12 clock hours per week; live meetings take a small part of it.

Questions and answers

What people usually ask before applying

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. The course uses ready-made prompt libraries and clear web services. The expert demonstrates complex setups such as ComfyUI at an overview level; you do not need to configure them yourself.

Do I need to complete Level 1 or know QGIS?+

No. QGIS is not part of the program, and the site data has been prepared. Graduates of the AI in Architecture course will find it easier to pick up the working rhythm, but it is not a requirement.

Is the program suitable for a property owner, an investor, or a realtor?+

Yes, Track B is designed for them. You do not need to design: you work with data and calculations and receive an investment concept brief for the property with a use hypothesis, a floor area balance, and basic economics. AI will draw for you to the extent needed for illustrations.

The certificate is issued to participants with higher education; others receive a certificate of attendance after the final review.

Which property should I take for the project?+

Your own real property: a building or a site you work with or would like to work with. If you do not have one, work with the base property C52.

What do I need for the classes?+

A computer, internet access, and active accounts in Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Paid subscriptions are not required; details on the services will be provided inside the course. Students independently provide access to the services required for learning in accordance with their user agreements and applicable law.

What if I miss a workshop?+

Workshops are recorded, and screencasts are available at any time. You can watch a missed meeting in the recording.

How much time does it take?+

72 academic hours over 4.5 weeks, about 12 clock hours per week. Most of the time goes to independent work on the project, and live meetings take a small share.

I do not have a higher education degree or I am still studying. What will I receive?+

You can join the program and present your project right away. After the final review you will receive a certificate of attendance, and the professional development certificate once you obtain a higher education degree.

Who pays 14,900 ₽?+

18,900 ₽ for everyone; 14,900 ₽ for graduates of the AI in Architecture course. The price is the same for both tracks.

Can I pay in installments?+

Yes, a two-installment plan is available: 50% at enrollment and the remaining 50% in the middle of the course. The terms are fixed when you sign the agreement with SFedU.

Can I get part of the money back through tax?+

Yes, if you pay yourself and have income subject to personal income tax, you can recover 13% of the cost. It is arranged through the tax office or your employer, using the agreement and the payment receipt.

What if I change my mind?+

The refund terms are set out in the agreement with SFedU. You sign the agreement before payment, so you can review the terms in advance. Questions about the agreement go to the SFedU coordinator: Alexander Andreev, alandreev@sfedu.ru.
