Who this course is for

An introductory course — no prior experience with AI tools

No prior AI knowledge is needed: we cover AI tools and QGIS from scratch, and you only need to be comfortable using a computer. You choose how deep to go in QGIS through one of two tracks.

Track A · I want to understand QGIS

Full immersion

You complete all three video lessons and build the base map and diagrams of your own site yourself. For those who want spatial analysis as a practical skill.

Track B · AI tools and the presentation matter more

Quick start

You watch only the QGIS introduction for a general understanding and don't need to install the software. Any site diagram from the internet will do for the presentation — there are no strict requirements.

QGIS is not a mandatory, heavy module you must complete to pass the course; it is optional depth you choose with your track. The final project review and the SFedU professional development certificate do not depend on which track you choose.

Who it suits and what you get

Students and beginners

You don't need a higher education degree to enroll: you can join and present your project right away. You enter the profession with a working set of AI tools and a finished project for your portfolio.

Architects, urban planners, environmental designers

Speed up pre-design analysis and visualization: site maps, scenarios, and a presentation for the client or review panel — in 2.5 weeks, without a heavy overhaul of your workflows.

Instructors in creative disciplines

Learn to spot where a student's work uses generative AI tools, and guide your students: AI is not a cheat code for gaming the system and getting a grade, but a working tool for high-quality architectural solutions.

There is no entrance exam. The professional development certificate is awarded to participants with higher education. If you don't have a degree yet, you can join and present your project right away: after the final review we issue a certificate of attendance, and the professional development certificate once you obtain your degree.

About the program · the problems it solves

Three places where site analysis most often stalls

01

Analysis takes days

Maps, diagrams, and descriptions have to be assembled and formatted by hand. In the course, diagrams are built in QGIS from open data, and AI helps formulate the conclusions.

02

AI is used blindly

A prompt like “come up with a park concept” produces a banal result. In the course you set the task using a template: role, context, task, examples, format, tone.

03

Conclusions are hard to present

The analysis stays in tables and files. In the course everything comes together in a single interactive presentation you can show to the client or review panel.

What you do in the course

A

You prepare site maps

Base maps, roads, buildings, terrain, cadastre. You style the map in QGIS and export it to PDF.

B

You analyze with AI

You assemble the brief and context, and build scenarios and audience profiles with language models.

C

You visualize and assemble the project

You turn sketches into visualizations with AI tools and assemble the result into a final presentation.

Role, context, task, examples, format, tone. Worked through in course assignments with AI tool results.

Open data

OpenStreetMap, cadastre, terrain data. QGIS is free, and no paid mapping software is needed.

05

Three live sessions

A review of your maps in QGIS, connecting geodata with AI tools, and the final project review, with independent work and chat tracking in between.

06

From scratch and no code

QGIS and AI tools are covered from scratch. No programming is needed.

The course uses QGIS, Photoshop, and services with language models. Paid subscriptions are not required: for the purposes of the course, Alice AI or another comparable local solution is enough. If you choose other services, such as Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude, the student provides access to them independently, in line with their user agreements and applicable law. The organizer does not provide communication tools or distribute tools for bypassing blocks.

Questions and answers

What people usually ask before enrolling

Do I need to know QGIS or AI tools?+

No. QGIS and work with language models are covered from scratch: the interface, a project, data, the prompt template. You only need to be comfortable using a computer.

Do I need to program?+

No, not a single line of code. You work in QGIS, AI web services, and Photoshop.

Which site should I take for the project?+

Your own: a park, a residential block, a university campus, a place you work with. If choosing is hard, you can take the Botanical Garden.

What do I need for the classes?+

A computer, internet access, QGIS (free), Photoshop on an Adobe subscription, and any service with a language model. Paid AI subscriptions are not required: for the purposes of the course you can use Alice AI or other comparable local solutions. We cover installing and setting up all the tools in the preparatory Sprint 0 module of the course guide.

If you choose international services, the student provides access to them independently, in line with their user agreements and applicable law.

How much time does it take?+

The program totals 22 academic hours over 2.5 weeks: 10 hours of practice in three evening live sessions and 12 hours of consultations and independent work in between, on a flexible schedule with tracking in the group chat. The exact cohort dates are in the schedule file we send after enrollment.

I don't have a higher education degree or I'm still studying. What will I get?+

You can join the course and submit your project right away. After the final assessment you receive a certificate of attendance, and the professional development certificate once you obtain your higher education degree.

Who pays 3,600 ₽?+

SFedU staff. All other participants pay 5,000 ₽. We will ask for proof of status after you apply.

Can I pay in installments?+

Yes, a two-installment plan is allowed: 50% at enrollment and the remaining 50% halfway through the course. The terms are fixed when you sign the agreement with SFedU.

Can I get part of the fee back via tax deduction?+

Yes, if you pay yourself and have income subject to personal income tax (NDFL): you can reclaim 13% of the fee. It is arranged through the tax office or your employer, using the agreement and the payment receipt.

What comes after the course?+

You can continue to Level 2, AI in Redevelopment: there you take a real property and develop it into a concept ready for the final review. 18,900 ₽; 14,900 ₽ for graduates of AI in Architecture. The price is the same for both tracks.

What if I change my mind?+

The refund terms are set out in the agreement with SFedU. You sign the agreement before paying, so you can review the terms in advance. Questions about the agreement: SFedU coordinator Alexander Andreev, alandreev@sfedu.ru.
