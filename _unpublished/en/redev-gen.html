Task A · Prompt

Prompt structure

Create an image series for an architectural project: [property and viewpoint]. Materials: [materials]. Light: [light]. Composition: [composition]. Style: [wording from the style dictionary]. Keep one structure for all frames in the series; change only the scene. Do not generate a new building from scratch: build on the source frame. Do not add details that are not in the style dictionary.

Structure: property and viewpoint + materials + light + composition + style dictionary.

Takeaway: The structure holds the series together, and the principle forbids generating a building from scratch.
