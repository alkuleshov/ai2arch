Why

Why Demo Day

Key points

  • the review shows whether the document withstands scrutiny;
  • the discussion explains what to strengthen;
  • the course is not completed without a review;
  • the output is feedback and the certificate.
Takeaway: Demo Day tests the document and the pitch, not the performance.
TermsRemote expert review: a review of the work based on the document and video pitch, without presenting at a board.
In short: experts read and watch without your narration.
Context

How the review works

Key points

  • experts review the document and the video pitch remotely;
  • the meeting is devoted to discussing the results;
  • the criteria are known in advance;
  • the review does not replace a project consultation.
Takeaway: The review is based on the document, and the meeting on the results.
TermsExpert review: an assessment of the work against given criteria.
In short: an assessment by clear rules.

Submission

What is submitted

Key points

  • the final document following the assembly map;
  • a five-minute video pitch;
  • Assignment 5 is mandatory;
  • any 3 of Assignments 1–4.
Takeaway: Assignment 5 is mandatory for everyone.
TermsAssignment 5: the final document and video pitch for the property.
In short: the course's final project.

How to receive the certificate

Key points

  • any 3 of Assignments 1–4;
  • Assignment 5 is mandatory;
  • the document and the pitch are submitted;
  • the course requirements are met.
Takeaway: The certificate is issued for meeting the requirements, not for the grade.

C52: a review example

Typical mistakes

Prompt

Prompt for a self-check

Act as a remote review expert. I have uploaded the final document and the video pitch statements. Check the work against the criteria: completeness against the assembly map; consistency of figures and visuals; honesty of the data; verifiability of the sources; clarity of the pitch. For each criterion, state what is confirmed, what is missing, and which question to ask. Do not add new facts.
Takeaway: The self-check shows where the document will not withstand the review.
